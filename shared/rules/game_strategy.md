# Holdet v3 — Game Strategy Notes
# Canonical truths about how this game is actually won.

## Captain selection
Captain can be changed every stage. It is a pure risk choice.
Top-3 stage finisher always beats GC leader on value unless stage is
a summit finish where GC leader is also stage winner.
Default: captain = highest P(win) rider on sprint stages.
On GC stages: captain = safest top-3 candidate, not the overall favorite.

## Stage depth bonus
Non-linear. Jumps sharply at 4+ riders in top 15.
On flat sprint stages with 6+ affordable sprint contenders in the field,
spreading budget across 8 plausible top-15 riders may beat
3 expensive riders + 5 passengers.
Depth bonus table: 0/4k/8k/15k/35k/65k/120k/220k/400k for 0–8 riders.

## Transfer cost
1% buy fee only. No exit fee. Free before Stage 1.
Gold tier = unlimited transfers.
Bank earns 0.5% interest per stage on cash held.

## DNF risk
DNF = −50k once + loss of all future earnings.
DNS = −100k per remaining stage (catastrophic).
High crash-risk riders on cobble/wet stages deserve explicit probability weight.

## GC penalty for sprinters
Sprinter in hard mountain stage → gruppetto → up to 30 min loss → −90k cap.
This is a structural sell pressure signal, not a probability input.

## Sprinter in mild hilly stage
Keeps GC time typically. Acceptable to hold.
Check stage profile before deciding to sell.
