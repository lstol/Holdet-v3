  - **DEEPER FINDING — the +128k delta is NOT a basin-search failure; it is a forward-proxy-change artifact.** Empirical apples-to-apples test (computed at probe end of V4b investigation):
    - Force-in propagates to `estimate_forward_costs` at [optimizer.py:741](claude/engine/optimizer.py:741), so when force_in=[Magnier], **team_n1 and team_n2 are also recomputed with Magnier included** — different forward proxies.
    - Under **identical** (unconstrained) forward proxies (`team_n1`/`team_n2` both Magnier-free): basin B (Magnier-out) `look_obj = +50,770`; basin C (Magnier-keep) `look_obj = −25,615`. **Basin C is 76k WORSE than basin B** given the same forward proxy.
    - Under **force-in** forward proxies (`team_n1`/`team_n2` include Magnier): basin C `look_obj = +147,653`.
    - **The +128k delta is the forward-proxy-recomputation effect** (147k − 50k ≈ 100k + small terms), not evidence of basin-search failure.
  - **What this means.** The unconstrained optimizer **correctly evaluates** Magnier-keep as worse under its own (consistent) objective. The SA chains don't reach Magnier-keep because Magnier-keep is genuinely the worse choice given the Magnier-free forward calibration. The user's force-in test was misleading as a diagnostic: it changed both the current-stage constraint AND the forward proxy, so the +128k delta conflated two effects.
  - **What about the original S17-ζ-redo finding of B1+B2+budget?** B1/B2/budget observations remain true as structural properties — random init is budget-greedy, biased-swap targets low-stage-EV slots, budget constraint blocks single-swap reach. But they're not the **load-bearing** mechanism for the Magnier case under unconstrained objective. The chains correctly avoid Magnier-keep because the unconstrained objective penalizes it. Even if SA reached basin C, the cross-chain selection by look_obj would correctly identify it as worse and discard it.
  - **Status: ⚠️ partial.** The three fixes are correct semantically and benign (don't break determinism; bit-identical in common cases; fix (d) is a real edge-case improvement). They do NOT resolve the user-observed Magnier gap because that gap is not a basin-search failure. **Recommend keeping the code changes** (correct directional improvements + low risk) and surfacing the asymmetric-forward-proxy issue as a new architectural item (**S17-θ** below).
  - **Acknowledged.** My S17-ζ-redo classification of "B1+B2 compound with budget amplifier" missed the forward-proxy-change effect. The classification described a real structural property of SA but misidentified it as the cause of the gap. The actual cause is the objective-formulation asymmetry. Pattern lesson: when comparing force-in vs unconstrained objective values, verify the forward proxies are identical before concluding "the constrained version is genuinely better under the same objective."
